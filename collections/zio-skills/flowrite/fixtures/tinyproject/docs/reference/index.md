Reference documentation for TinyProject's two modules is being written.

## `optics`

- **`Iso`** — a lossless, reversible conversion.
- **`Lens`** — focus on a part that is always present.
- **`Prism`** — focus on one case of a sum type.
- **`Optional`** — focus on a part that may be absent but can be replaced.

## `tally`

- **`Ledger`** — an append-only count of named events.
- **`Window`** — a bounded view of the most recent readings.
