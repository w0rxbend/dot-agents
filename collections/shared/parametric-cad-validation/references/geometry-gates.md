# Geometry and interface gates

Reviewed [Tigerbeetle geometry validators](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbeetle%20V2/src/fpv_frame/validation/geometry.py) separate planar profiles from physical components. Profile needs one valid face, closed wires, positive area, expected hole count and supporting XY plane at Z=0. Physical component needs one valid closed manifold solid and positive volume. Keep that split; do not apply one “shape exists” check to both. OCCT bounding boxes pad curved edges with tolerance margins; check stock planes against actual supporting geometry.

Measure shared interfaces on placed solids, independent of their constructor: coaxial bores, usable through-holes, contact surfaces, symmetry, root coverage, edge ligaments, required clearances. Circular rim at each end does not prove unobstructed bore. [Mechanical regression suite](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbeetle%20V2/tests/assembly/test_mechanical_gates.py) deliberately detects internal plug with both rims open. Equipment envelopes must clear material and each other. When dimensions are absent, keep “unverified equipment”; do not count missing measurements as passed clearance.

[Tigerbee validation](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbee/src/tigerbee/validation.py) measures motor axes, symmetry and material interfaces on actual placed components. Do not transfer its dimensional constants to Tigerbeetle or another frame. Keep millimetres, square/cubic units and numerical tolerances attached to their checks. Numerical mass-property tolerance differs from manufacturing allowance and equipment clearance.

Tigerbeetle V2 [quality workflow](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/.github/workflows/tigerbeetle-v2-ci.yml) uses frozen uv dependencies, Ruff, mypy, pytest and reference/default/minimum_supported/maximum_supported/tolerance_test preset validation. Tigerbee's [CI contract](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/.github/workflows/tigerbee.yml) differs and includes committed-export freshness, all components, assembly --require-fit and accessory interfaces. Follow changed project's gates. Topological and fit checks do not certify load strength; keep report's actual scope.
