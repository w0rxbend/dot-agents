# Geometry and interface gates

The reviewed [Tigerbeetle geometry validators](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbeetle%20V2/src/fpv_frame/validation/geometry.py) distinguish planar profiles from physical components. A profile requires one valid face, closed wires, positive area, the expected hole count and a supporting XY plane at Z=0. A physical component requires one valid closed manifold solid and positive volume. Preserve those distinctions rather than applying one “shape exists” check to both. OCCT bounding boxes include tolerance margins around curved edges; use actual supporting geometry when checking stock planes.

Measure shared interfaces on placed solids independently of the constructor that produced them: coaxial bores, usable through-holes, contact surfaces, symmetry, root coverage, edge ligaments and required clearances. A circular rim at each end does not prove an unobstructed bore; the [mechanical regression suite](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbeetle%20V2/tests/assembly/test_mechanical_gates.py) intentionally detects an internal plug with both rims open. Equipment envelopes must clear the material and one another. Preserve “unverified equipment” when dimensions are absent rather than turning missing measurements into a passed clearance.

[Tigerbee validation](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/Tigerbee/src/tigerbee/validation.py) measures motor axes, symmetry and material interfaces on actual placed components. Do not transfer its dimensional constants to Tigerbeetle or another frame. Keep millimetres, square/cubic units and numerical tolerances attached to their checks. Numerical mass-property tolerance is distinct from manufacturing allowance and equipment clearance.

For Tigerbeetle V2, the [quality workflow](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/.github/workflows/tigerbeetle-v2-ci.yml) uses frozen uv dependencies, Ruff, mypy, pytest and reference/default/minimum_supported/maximum_supported/tolerance_test preset validation. Tigerbee has a different [CI contract](https://github.com/w0rxbend/FreeCAD-Projects/blob/826231b01444c893c95c819e9e759f4b6f520b4d/.github/workflows/tigerbee.yml), including committed-export freshness, all components, assembly --require-fit and accessory interfaces. Follow the changed project's gates. Topological and fit checks do not certify load strength; retain the report's actual scope.
